## Flight lesson 2.3 - Solo 2

| Lesson number | Lesson name | Lesson date(s) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.3 | Solo 2 | 28-09-2026 |

The flight lesson 2.3 with name Solo 2 focusses on basic flight procedures being alone in the cockpit. In phase 1 I learned how to fly, take-off and land the plane with some turns, stalls and recovery procedures baked in. In the first solo we break the seal of flying solo for the first time, without any form of help in the cockpit. The second solo flight is somewhat more complex and wants you to:

- Take-off
- Leave the circuit
- Perform some 30 degrees and 45 degrees (steep) turns
- Rejoin the circuit
- Perform 1 or 2 touch and go's
- Make a full stop landing

This is really a very basic flight where most aspects will come into play, as well as every memory item, procedure and radio call must be performed. A great way to step up the game in solo flight.

## The weather during this lesson

The weather during this lesson was great. We had a sky filled with some high level stratus clouds with some cumulus clouds under it. We got some half sunlight because of the layer of (also)stratus. The wind was somewhat variable but stable on our runway. No big crosswind challenges here.

## First the lesson with instructor

As we are going solo with relative little experience and after a week of not flying, we will first do the lesson with instructor. Then he gets out and wishes me luck to repeat everything on my own.

With instructor, everything went okay. Some little remarks here and there like climbing in turns, speed too little and too less or much bank angle. No big deals. Then we did 2 circuits where I pulled off my best landing so far. We almost did not feel any touchdown and this is where I want to be at all landings. The landing after that, we had to make big turns because of other traffic, slow flying into downwind and base and resulting in us too high and a somewhat unstable landing. Could have been a go around.

Then my instructor got out and wished me luck to repeat everything.

## Flying the Solo 2 lesson

I went back to the holding point of the runway and took off with no remarks. This went perfect and it amazes me again how much the plane wants to climb with the less weight without instructor. I climbed out of the circuit and left the circuit. Then I did the after take-off checklist on my own which is a strange experience, after doing this about 20-25 times with instructor.

I flew to a nearby village at 10 kilometers away to perform the steep turns. I decided to perform two turns, one over left and one over right.

To review the steep turn exercise a bit, we bank just like any other turn but at 30 degrees we add around 200-300 RPM and hold the plane on a specific altitude. In my case 1.500 feet. Both turns went great in my opinion, with the turn over the left I climbed around 50 feet in altitude. I corrected this in the right turn.

Then I went back to the circuit, and some traffic was already in the pattern. Therefore I left the circuit as my instructor has done also one or two times and rejoined with a big and sweeping curve.

In the circuit, everything went allright but my landings were a little bit too hard on touchdown. Both of them. I need to get the correct flare height and amount of yoke input somewhat better. My instructor saw the landings from half a mile and marked them as OK, but not perfect but I found them worse.

## Remarks

Of course, Im a little bit of a perfectionist so every lesson has some remarks:

- Getting the right flare height and amount of input
- No altitude changes in turns, keep the plane on the desired altitude
