| 2.4 | UPRT 1 | 19-10-2026 |

The flight lesson 2.4 with name UPRT 1 is an advanced flight lesson where we learn some Upset Prevention and Recovery Training (UPRT). This means we will be trained to recognize the plane getting in a spin or spiral dive and be able to recover successfully. Getting in those situations can happen, and be better prepared and know what to do than facing this and completely lose control.

Another core fundament learned in this lesson is to be able to recover from various inputs my instructor does as passengers can also do unexpected actions. Learn to act and react.

## 19-10-2026

Today we tried to perform this flight lesson. However, we were not expecting Visual Meteorological Conditions (VMC), where we need the cloud base at atleast 5.000ft and 10KM of visibility.

The weather was very detoriating today where it started very cloudy, clouds dissapearing for about 1.5 hours but then a cloud base again at around 1.600ft.

The METAR of a nearby aerodrome published:

`METAR XXXX 191355Z AUTO 11011KT 9999 BKN016 12/08 Q1027=`

Broken clouds at 1.600 feet means a cloud coverage of at least 5/8 which is not good for VFR flying and the best way to fly is not to fly.

Lesson cancelled, looking forward to the next lesson!
