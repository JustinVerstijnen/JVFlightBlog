## Flight lesson 2.9 - Solo 5

| Lesson number | Lesson name | Lesson date(s) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.9 | Solo 5 | 06-11-2026 |

The flight lesson 2.9 with name Solo 5 focusses again on basic flight procedures being alone in the cockpit. In this lesson, I'm supposed to perform these exercises at least:

- Take-off
- Leave the circuit
- Perform 2 steep turns over right
- Perform 2 steep turns over left
- Rejoin the circuit
- Make at least 2 landings

## The weather during this lesson

The weather during this lesson was beautiful, with some minor winds from the east and some moist air but an overall clear blue sky without clouds. The visibility was good on the ground but in the air and into the sun somewhat poor and was something to take into account but not stopping from flying totday.

## Flying the Solo 5 lesson

We started the lesson with a quick briefing of all the exercises that needed to be done. I got a list on paper which I could check off when in the air and flying the exercises step by step which I did. We first did 3 circuits to refresh the feeling of controlling an airplane again, which is a great thing I want to keep in the rythm for now. As this was my first lesson in about 3 weeks I want to keep this habit to refresh my skills and some references.

Then the instructor got out and gave it a go to fly alone which I did.

---

## The solo exercises

I will dive deeper in all the exercises I had to do, and will write down what went good and what can be enhanced further to learn from this experience. I took off, left the circuit and climbed to 2.500ft. This is a good altitude for performing various exercises, and I wanted to be on a nice and round number.

### Steep turns over left

Then after fully trimmed and performed the after take-off checklist I started the steep turn exercises which I alternated from left to right and then again left and right to keep this continuity. The first steep turn went good with a minor increase in altitude (about 50 feet). Then leveled off to do the right turn.

### Steep turns over right

Then I performed the steep turn over right, as we have less good visibility over right as we are seated left. This also went okay, and learning to fly purely on cockpit visuals and keeping this in a certain way during the whole turn.

In between the turns I was looking out for other traffic where I saw two other planes in the vicinity and took a corrective steering action to the right to not hit each others flight paths but no dangerous situations here.

---

### Landing(s)

Then I went back to the circuit to do about 4 circuits and train myself to land the plane better, also with no live instructions about getting high on final or corrections in steerings.

The first landing I touched down a bit too hard resulting in a bounce, and I learned that a bounce is a go-around which I did immediately. Small review on going around as I forgot the flaps:

Going around:

1. Full Power
2. Carb Heat off (this is one action)
3. Flaps to 20
4. Keep the plane stable and climb similar to a take-off and slowly out of ground effect

The second landing I again hit the ground a bit too early but correct it by flaring the plane. Then it got on the ground in an okay manner. Then I set the flaps to 10 and took off again for a third circuit.

On the third landing I was once again too soft on the flare but eventually pulled back to make my best solo landing so far. Happy about that and set the flaps to 10 to take off again for another round.

The fourth landing I wanted to flare better but resulted in flaring a bit too high making the plane climb, so I pushed it down for a bit and flared again and got on the ground. Not very bad but worse than landing 3.

---

## Remarks

Overall it was a great lesson in my opinion. M my instructor was keeping a lookout during the landings and gave me tips about how we can improve on landings:

- I have a tendency to be too high on final, I need to descend more firm and keep 65 knots (at least)
- We need to get the flare altitude better which helps in flaring longer and more subtle
- Landings where the nose wheel also hits the ground on touchdown are forbidden (he said with a laugh)

I think overall airwork, checklists, briefings and circuit flying went great but it is mainly the landings where I want to improve a lot.
