## Flight lesson 2.7 - Solo 4

| Lesson number | Lesson name | Lesson date(s) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.7 | Solo 4 | 06-10-2026 |

The flight lesson 2.7 with name Solo 7 focusses again on basic flight procedures being alone in the cockpit. In this lesson, I'm supposed to perform these exercises at least:

- Take-off
- Leave the circuit
- Performing Slow Flight
- Perform a approach to stall in approach configuration (Flaps 20)
- Perform a approach to stall in landing configuration (Flaps 30)
- Perform a full stall in approach configuration (Flaps 20)
- Perform a full stall in landing configuration (Flaps 30)
- Rejoin the circuit
- Make a full stop landing (or touch and go if possible)

As this is a future lesson, I want to use this lesson to further enhance my solo landings. Till now, landings with instructor went great but on solo they could be much better.
